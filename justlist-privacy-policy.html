Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

1. About JustList

JustList is a minimalist list and checklist app developed by Volodymyr Lisovyi in Poland. This policy explains how information is handled when you create multiple lists, add, edit or delete tasks, mark tasks complete, use filters and bulk completion, or manage settings.

2. Information we handle

JustList handles the list names, task text, completion status and preferences you provide to deliver its checklist features. Depending on your settings and interactions with us, we may also receive app analytics and diagnostic information, and your email address and correspondence when you contact support.

We use list and task information to operate the app, analytics and diagnostics to understand performance and improve reliability, and support correspondence to respond to questions and resolve issues.

3. Private iCloud storage

JustList uses Apple’s private iCloud storage for list and task data. “Private” refers to storage associated with your iCloud account rather than a publicly shared database; it is not a promise of a particular encryption method or that no service provider can process the data.

Where optional iCloud sync is enabled, supported devices can synchronize information using Apple’s services. Availability depends on your Apple account, device settings, connectivity and available iCloud storage. Device copies or caches may also be retained to support app operation; this policy does not promise that content is stored exclusively on the device or exclusively in iCloud.

Apple processes information under its own terms and Privacy Policy. Sync and backups are distinct: disabling sync does not necessarily erase information already stored in iCloud or backups.

4. Analytics and diagnostics

We may receive app usage, performance and crash information through Apple’s analytics and diagnostic services, depending on the information Apple makes available and your sharing settings. This information helps identify errors and improve JustList. You can review the analytics and privacy controls available in your device settings.

JustList does not integrate other third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. This policy does not treat analytics as necessarily anonymous or promise that all diagnostic information is free of personal information.

5. Email and support

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond to you, investigate issues and maintain relevant support records. Please send only information needed to explain your request.

Your support message is separate from your private iCloud list storage. Deleting a task or uninstalling JustList does not delete an email you have sent to us.

6. Sharing and service providers

JustList uses Apple’s services for private iCloud storage and available app analytics and diagnostics. No other third-party services are integrated into the app. Email correspondence is processed through the email systems used to send and receive it.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to respond to lawful requests or protect legal rights. This policy does not authorize use of your list content for unrelated purposes.

7. Retention and deletion

List and task information is retained to provide the app’s functionality until you remove it using the available controls. You can edit or delete tasks and lists in JustList. Marking tasks complete, including in bulk, changes their status rather than deleting them.

Cloud changes can take time to synchronize. Device copies, cloud records and backups may have different retention and recovery behavior. Uninstalling the app or disabling sync does not necessarily delete cloud data or backups. Review your Apple account’s storage and backup controls and contact us if you need help understanding the available deletion options.

Analytics and diagnostic information is retained according to the applicable Apple service arrangements and any records retained by the developer for troubleshooting. Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of personal information we hold, subject to those obligations.

8. Security

Protect your device, Apple account and backups. Private cloud storage does not eliminate all security risks. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact the developer using the details below to exercise rights concerning information the developer holds.

Processing is carried out as necessary to provide requested app functions and support, for legitimate interests in maintaining reliability and responding to inquiries, to meet legal obligations, or on the basis of consent where required. The applicable basis depends on the information and purpose involved.

Apple’s processing locations and any international transfers are governed by its applicable service terms and privacy disclosures. Support email may be processed in countries where the email providers operate, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

10. Children’s privacy

JustList is a general-purpose checklist tool. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate steps under applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when JustList or our data practices change. The revised version will show an updated effective date. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required by applicable law.
