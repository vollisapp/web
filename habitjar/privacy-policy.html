Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitJar (the “App”) handles information when you use it. HabitJar is designed to help you create habits, manage reminders, track progress, and record positive moments.

1. Information stored in the App

The App may store information that you enter, such as habit names, schedules, completion history, reminder preferences, notes, and positive moments added to your jar.

By default, this information is stored locally on your device. We do not receive or access this content unless you choose to send it to us, for example when contacting support.

2. Information we collect

HabitJar does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through its own servers. The App does not sell personal information and does not use your information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App will request permission to send local notifications. Notification schedules and related settings are processed on your device. You can change notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

If HabitJar offers paid features or subscriptions, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment-card information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies and terms.

5. Diagnostics and third-party services

Apple may provide developers with aggregated or privacy-protected diagnostic information, depending on your device and App Store privacy settings. HabitJar does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

If analytics, cloud synchronization, crash reporting, or other third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before those services are used where required.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored App data remains on your device until you delete it within the App, reset the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App normally removes its locally stored data, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.

Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

7. Children’s privacy

HabitJar is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required by applicable law for a person to provide consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the App. However, no device, storage method, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please protect access to your device and backups.

9. International users

You may use HabitJar in different countries. Because the App’s core content is stored locally, we generally do not transfer that content to our servers. Any information you voluntarily send to support may be processed in the country where the developer or service provider operates.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. Because we generally do not possess your locally stored HabitJar content, you can usually exercise control directly through the App or by deleting the App.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or our practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
